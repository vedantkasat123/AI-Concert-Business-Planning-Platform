# ABC Events – Promoter AI
## Enterprise AI Decision Intelligence Platform

*A clickable enterprise prototype demonstrating an AI-assisted decision support platform for concert promoters.*

---

# 📌 Project Overview

**Promoter AI** is an enterprise AI decision intelligence platform designed to assist concert promoters throughout the business planning lifecycle.

The prototype demonstrates how AI can transform promoter decision-making by combining historical deal intelligence, codified leadership expertise, business rules, and explainable AI recommendations into a single enterprise experience.

The objective is to improve business plan quality before leadership review while preserving human governance and decision ownership.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Concert promoters rely heavily on senior leadership to validate business plans before deals are approved.

Current challenges include:

- Multiple review and revision cycles
- Limited access to historical deal intelligence
- Knowledge concentrated within senior leaders
- Inconsistent planning decisions
- Time-consuming manual reviews
- Difficulty scaling organizational expertise

The proposed solution addresses these challenges through AI-assisted, evidence-based decision support.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

Promoter AI institutionalizes organizational expertise by combining:

- Historical Deal Intelligence
- Codified Leadership Knowledge
- Business Rules
- Explainable AI
- Human-in-the-Loop Governance

The platform provides promoters with contextual recommendations before plans are submitted for executive approval.

Leadership always retains final approval authority.

# Signature Feature

## Persistent AI Reasoning Panel

The prototype includes a persistent AI Reasoning Panel that remains visible across the application.

It provides complete transparency into the AI's reasoning by displaying:

- Current Objective
- Active AI Workflow
- Evidence Retrieved
- Historical Comparables
- Leadership Principles
- Business Rules Applied
- Confidence Score
- Business Impact
- Recommendations
- Suggested Next Actions

This creates an explainable AI experience that builds user trust.

# 🛡 Governance Principles

- AI augments human decision-making.
- Leadership retains final approval authority.
- Every recommendation is evidence-based.
- Confidence scoring accompanies recommendations.
- AI never fabricates information.
- Human review is mandatory for external communications.
- Organizational knowledge is curated through structured governance.
